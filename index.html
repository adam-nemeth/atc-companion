Download from sensorticked sensors

Done once your readings are saved. Together, they make the sensor's new log start with a correct clock, so clock drift can be corrected. For just one, use “Set clock now” or “Erase sensor log…”“Set clocks” below, or “Erase sensor log…” on the Sensor tab.

A sensor's clock can run fast or slow. The clock is read before downloading and, if it's off, the readings since the last clock set are shifted to the right time; the sensor's own times stay in the sensor_timestamp column. It works best with “Set clock & erase” ticked, because the log then always starts right after a clock set.

Sensor configuration

Bluetooth name (shown in the Bluetooth picker)

Connect to a sensor to see its Bluetooth name.

Up to 16 letters, digits, _ or -. “ATC_” is added in front if you leave it out (the Connect picker only lists names starting with it). The sensor applies the new name after you disconnect; the iPhone may keep showing the old name until Bluetooth is turned off and on.

Settings

Change values, then tap “Write config”.

They are read from the sensor when you connect, and nothing is sent before you tap “Write config”. Settings that say “leave as it is” mean different things on different models or firmware versions.
